Your Job to Survive

Animated survival stories told from your point of view: how useful is your job when the world breaks?

Every video follows one ordinary profession (a firefighter, a police officer, a security guard, a pilot…) through sixty days of a world that has stopped working. The skills of the job decide who gets through the first night, the first month and the way out. Each story is one long video, with a short for every part.
